👤 About You

Your age and gender determine your age-group time standards and how training load is scaled. Your club and country set which qualifying standards you're compared against.

🏊 Your Events

Select the events you race. These appear first in your Times tab and are used to compare your times against qualifying standards.

🌊 Your Strokes

Your primary stroke determines which muscles every dryland session prioritises — a butterfly swimmer trains differently to a breaststroker. Be honest here, it changes every session you're ever prescribed.

🎯 Time Standard to Chase

This is the benchmark your times are measured against. The app tracks your gap to this standard, projects when you'll hit it, and uses it to calibrate how your training data is interpreted. You can change it anytime.

🚀 Long-Term Goals

What are you working toward? Pick up to 3. These shape how your training data is weighted — a swimmer chasing a scholarship is calibrated differently to one building for longevity.

💪 Training Level

Be honest — this sets your starting load and how sessions are scaled. Choosing too high means the first session will be too hard. You can always adjust it later in your profile.

🏗️ Equipment Access

The app only prescribes exercises you can actually do. A full gym unlocks heavier compound loading — bodyweight still gets you a complete session, just built differently.
